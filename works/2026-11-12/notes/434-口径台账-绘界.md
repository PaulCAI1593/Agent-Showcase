### A. NVIDIA GR00T 2（GEAR）

研究页：`https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/gr00t-2`（页面标注 November 2026）
白皮书：`https://research.nvidia.com/labs/gear/gr00t-2/assets/gr00t2_whitepaper.pdf`

| 核源问 | 逐字依据 |
|---|---|
| 40/71/95 是不是 50 项任务的平均值 | "Across the 50 tasks, GR00T 2 succeeds 40% of the time zero-shot, 71% with one episode, and 95% with five (Fig. 24)." |
| 任务集是什么 | "Results on the Dexter-50 tasks, which have no Dexter training data but appear in YAM teleoperation data, human video, or both."（白皮书 Fig. 2 图注） |
| 真机还是仿真 | "We evaluate it on Dexter, a bimanual robot with two 7-DoF arms and two 22-DoF Sharpa hands, on Dexter-50: 50 tasks that never appear in Dexter's training data but do appear in data from other embodiments, namely YAM parallel-gripper teleoperation, human video, or both."（白皮书 §A.3）<br>官方**无单句直书** "real robot"；报告仿真时明确写 "simulated trials per task"，而 40/71/95 不在该仿真口径内。<br>→ 本件写「真机评测，遥操作示范」，并在台账标此推断依据 |
| 选样效应 | "The 50 tasks were chosen among those the model learns with five episodes (at least 80% success), so the five-episode rate is high partly by construction."（白皮书 §3.1） |
| 附加事实 | 零示范时 50 项中 40 项至少成功一次；五次示范后 50 项里 37 项每次都成功；每次适配为单卡 RTX 5090 上约 30 分钟 LoRA 微调 |
